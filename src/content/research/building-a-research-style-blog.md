A research-style blog is not just a chronological feed. It is a public knowledge
system with enough structure to help readers understand what belongs together.

For this site, the first useful structure is simple:

- featured essays
- publication list
- topic pages
- short notes
- clear article metadata

The goal is not to make the system heavy. The goal is to make future writing
easy to place.
